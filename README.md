# Marvel timeline

Every Marvel Studios film and series as one map: titles linked in watch order by the characters they share. Search, filter by character or phase, switch between graph and mind-map layouts, and see the path from the first film to any title.

Live: https://mdnihal5.github.io/marvel-timeline/

One file, no build step. A fan project, not affiliated with Marvel or Disney. Data is a hand-made sample and may be out of date.
